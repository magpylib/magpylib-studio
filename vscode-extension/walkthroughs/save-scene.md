# Save your scene

A scene is a `.magpy.json` file, open in a tab of its own. **Save**, **Save As**
and **Revert** work as for any file, each scene keeps its own undo, and several
can be open side by side.

It opens again as you left it, variables, variants and steps included, and
opening it never runs code. Unsaved work survives a crash: the studio keeps a
recovery copy and offers it back.
